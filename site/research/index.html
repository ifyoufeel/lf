    HRT — 04 · Mechanism

    How El Niño reaches a field.

    Warm water spreads east and the rising air follows it. Rain belts shift. What it costs a harvest depends on the crop’s stage when the rain fails.

    After Ropelewski and Halpert (1987) and Iizumi et al. (2014).
