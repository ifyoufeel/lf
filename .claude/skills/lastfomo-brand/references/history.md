## Decisions in order

1. **Copy (v1 to v2):** invented results, "EST. 2026", the "By appointment" chip and the invented field-report page were removed. Feeding systems and hardware became one discipline. Pixel type was limited to brand headers. Footage was desaturated so the wing colour leads.
2. **Formats:** v3 kept everything in the centre square so one master yields 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16. v4 kept everything in the middle band (y 360 to 720) with no logos top or bottom.
3. **The horse:** never cropped. All film endings were remade to match the Arca v2 ending, with the whole horse in a padded plate.
4. **Loops:** the main vibe, little text, centred, about 5 s, under 1 MB. Then content 33% smaller, then 22% smaller with better quality, then 11% smaller at 1920 x 1080 and crisp, still under 1 MB.
5. **Thumbnails and social:** minimal and on brand, readable at small sizes, no text-heavy banners.
6. **Landing page:** scroll by scroll, showreels in place, a page per case with metrics and scope. From comments: Arca consistent on mobile, the same arch holder for every wing, the approved loops in the holders, and a Start a project page.
7. **Walking experience:** PolyFauna-like portals to three worlds with interactive objects and ambient sound. From comments: Hortus and Arca restyled in Machina's dark neon look with a per-wing grade.
8. **Walkthrough film:** real captures of the site and real gameplay, edited with brand cards.
9. **Sound field:** the walking experience's jam machine (seven rune stones, one jam in A minor, scenes) moved into the landing page's walk as its reward: the stones rise in the house once all three wings are explored. The owner tried it in a review Artifact and approved it to go live, wording included (27 October 2026). Next, by the owner's request: more loops per stone and more scenes.
