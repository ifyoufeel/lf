# Copy and facts

Contents: voice, approved lines, wing copy, the facts ledger, things removed on request, open placeholders, rights.

## Voice

Elegant, minimal, airy, sharp and deliberate: brutalism with a corporate note. Short declaratives, often in threes. No hype, no exclamation marks, no superlatives, no emoji. Say what the house does and what is true about it, then stop.

Practical habits:
- Lines of two to six words for supers and headlines. One idea each.
- British spelling (optimisation, colour, programme).
- Typographic punctuation in visible text: ’ “ ” and the em dash in labels (`MCH — Machina`). Straight quotes only in code.
- Mono strips separate items with ` / ` or ` · ` in uppercase, for example `8 GPU / 176 GB VRAM / 64 cores / 0 bytes off-site`.
- Lowercase brand and wing names in wordmarks (lastfomo, machina); capitalised in running text (Machina, Hortus, Arca).
